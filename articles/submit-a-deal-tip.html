Spotted a deal? Send us a tip — here's how

By Deals & Days Out

Deal card by Deals & Days Out

The best deals we post come from readers like you. If you spot something good, send it our way — we'll verify it and post the best ones.

What makes a great tip

  • A real price drop — the item, the store, and the price you saw (a screenshot or link helps).
  • A freebie — free samples, free-after-rebate offers, or legit giveaways.
  • A family event or day out — especially free or cheap ones. Local Michigan events are our sweet spot, but great national finds work too.

How to send it

The fastest way: message our Facebook page with the details — what it is, where you saw it, and the price. That's it.

What happens next

We check every tip before posting: is the price still live? Is the offer legit? If it checks out, it goes up on the page and the site — with credit to you if you'd like (just say so in your message), or anonymously if you prefer.

One rule: please only send deals you've actually seen yourself — no rumors, no "a friend said." We verify everything, and real sightings save everyone time.
