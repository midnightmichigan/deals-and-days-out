Spotted a deal? Send us a tip

See a price drop, a freebie, or a great family event? Send it our way — the best tips get posted, with credit if you want it.

What makes a good tip

  • What the deal is, and where — the store or site, and the exact item or event
  • The price you saw, and what it normally costs if you know it
  • A link to the deal or event page, if there is one
  • When it ends, if it is time-limited

How to send it

Message our Facebook page: Deals & Days Out. That is the only tip channel we monitor — please don't send personal information about yourself or anyone else.

What happens next

Every tip is checked before anything is posted: we verify the price or event ourselves, and we never state a price we couldn't verify. If a deal dies or a price changes after posting, we update the post. We don't pay for tips.
